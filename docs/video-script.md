# KalaApi: 30-second video script

Format: screen capture of the live app + voice-over (or text on screen). English subtitles are required.
Live app: https://6-scrambled-eggs.github.io/KalaApi/
Record at 1280x720 or larger, browser zoom 100%, language set to EN. Record the backup capture too (PRD: App QA).
**Confirm the team name** before you record. The text below uses "6 Scrambled Eggs" (the GitHub organization name).

| Sec | On screen | Voice-over / subtitle |
|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | Title card: team name + "Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots" | "We are 6 Scrambled Eggs. Our challenge: harmonize MODIS and VIIRS hot spots." |
| 4-10 | App page `#/surge/indonesia`. Hold on the two big numbers: **x6.5 raw counts** and **x1.2 harmonized**. | "In 2012, Indonesia's fire count jumped six and a half times. The fires did not. A new satellite sensor came in. Harmonized, the jump is only 1.2 times." |
| 10-14 | Title card: **KalaApi** + "One fire record for Indonesia" | "This is KalaApi. One fire record for Indonesia." |
| 14-24 | Open `#/area/kalimantan-barat`. Calendar appears. Point to the 2026 row (red El Nino dot) and the flagged weeks list (week 43, 100th percentile). Click "Story mode". | "Pick any province. See 25 years of burning on one calendar. 2026 is flagged: West Kalimantan reached the 100th percentile in week 43." |
| 24-30 | Story mode chart for Papua Selatan, 2026 line above 2015. Then cut to `#/brief/papua-selatan` (readiness window tile). | "Is 2026 worse than 2015? Not in all of Indonesia, but in South Papua and West Kalimantan, yes. And KalaApi tells BPBD when to be ready: a one-page brief for every area." |

## Facts behind each line (all from the app data, week 43 of 2026)
- National fire-days, 2026 vs 2015, same weeks: 130,628 vs 171,995 (**76% of 2015**). FRP: 69%.
- South Papua: 17,977 vs 15,484 (**116% of 2015**; about 4.5 times 2019).
- West Kalimantan: 17,869 vs 15,915 (**112% of 2015**). 2026 flagged weeks: 3-5, 12, 13 and 43.
- Central Kalimantan 67%, South Sumatra 43%, Riau 13% of 2015.
- Fire-days are heat detections. They are not burned area. Do not say "hectares" in the video.

## Checks before you record
1. Hard-refresh the site (Ctrl+Shift+R). Open each page once so it loads fast.
2. Say "x6.5" and "x1.2" only if the surge page still shows them (it did on 14 Nov).
3. If you add AI-made images or voice, add a watermark or caption "AI-generated". No NASA logo.
4. Add English subtitles (burn them in or upload an .srt file).
